Mediterranean Power Bowl

A colorful bowl packed with chickpeas, fresh vegetables, quinoa and creamy yogurt dressing.

⏱ 20 min 👥 2 servings ⭐ Easy
NUTRITION FACTS
Per serving
Calories 420 kcal
Protein 24g
Carbohydrates 48g
Fat 16g
Fiber 10g
🧾 Ingredients
  • 100g cooked quinoa
  • 120g chickpeas
  • 1 cucumber
  • 100g cherry tomatoes
  • 40g feta cheese
  • Greek yogurt
  • Lemon juice
  • Fresh herbs
📋 Instructions
  1. Cook quinoa according to package instructions.
  2. Chop cucumber and tomatoes.
  3. Add quinoa and chickpeas to a bowl.
  4. Top with vegetables and feta.
  5. Mix yogurt with lemon juice and herbs.
  6. Drizzle dressing over the bowl and serve.

Garlic Chicken & Veggies

Juicy garlic chicken served with roasted vegetables and crispy baby potatoes.

⏱ 35 min 👥 2 servings ⭐ Easy
NUTRITION FACTS
Per serving
Calories 465 kcal
Protein 48g
Carbohydrates 31g
Fat 17g
Fiber 7g
🧾 Ingredients
  • 250g chicken breast
  • 200g baby potatoes
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Italian herbs
📋 Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Chop potatoes and vegetables.
  3. Season chicken with garlic and herbs.
  4. Place everything on a baking tray.
  5. Roast for approximately 30–35 minutes.

Chocolate Protein Cake

A soft chocolate cake with extra protein and a rich chocolate flavor.

⏱ 30 min 👥 6 servings ⭐ Medium
NUTRITION FACTS
Per serving
Calories 245 kcal
Protein 18g
Carbohydrates 28g
Fat 8g
Fiber 4g
🧾 Ingredients
  • 2 bananas
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g oat flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 40g protein powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Dark chocolate chips
📋 Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mash bananas in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs and mix well.
  4. Add remaining ingredients.
  5. Pour into a small cake tin.
  6. Bake for approximately 25–30 minutes.
